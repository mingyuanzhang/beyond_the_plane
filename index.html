A DIFFERENT WAY OF SEEING

You live inside a plane.

A solid passing through your world creates a 2D cross-section. Because your eyes live in that same plane, you see its edges as a strip of colored light.

  1. Follow time. Watch the strip grow, shrink, split, or stay the same. Drag the timeline to any instant.
  2. Freeze and walk. Pause time, then circle the slice. A square and a circle can look similar from one direction.
  3. Make a hypothesis. Build a mental picture first. Select Ready to guess to see the candidates, then choose one and reveal the answer. You can close the choices and keep observing at any time.

All powers are yours from the beginning. Every object travels straight through your world at the same speed. Your walking distance stays fixed; brighter edges face you more directly. Candidate colors are identical, so shape is the clue.

Keyboard: Space to pause/resume; A/D or left/right arrows to walk; R to reverse; [ and ] to step time. Range sliders also support their usual arrow keys. On touch, use the sliders or hold the movement buttons.
